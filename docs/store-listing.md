# Store listing package

## Product copy

**App name:** PlanIT

**Short description:** Private, offline-capable money tracking with an auditable ledger.

**Full description:**

PlanIT helps you understand where your money is, where it went, and what is coming next.
Track accounts, expenses, income, transfers, debts, shared costs, reimbursements, recurring
commitments, and savings goals in one consistent financial ledger.

Use PlanIT offline and keep entering transactions; queued changes synchronize safely when
the connection returns. Explore cash flow, spending, merchants, products, and price history,
with clear explanations when exchange-rate information is missing. Export your records at
any time and delete your profile from inside the app.

PlanIT never treats transfers as spending, never silently changes posted transactions, and
never requires a paid OCR or bank-connection service. Manual entry remains fully supported.

## Store submission facts

- Category: Finance
- Ads: none
- In-app purchases: none in version 0.10.0
- Account creation: required for server synchronization
- Account deletion: available in Settings inside the app
- Financial features: tracking and personal organization only; no lending, brokerage,
  payments, investment execution, or financial advice
- Target audience: general adult audience; complete the final age-rating questionnaire in
  each store portal
